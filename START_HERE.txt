WHY YOU SEE ONLY LLM TOOLS:

The tools (/api/tools endpoint) are just DEFINITIONS.
They tell you what tools COULD be available.

But Claude is only INVOKED when you actually CALL Claude
via one of these endpoints:
  • POST /api/llm/chat
  • POST /api/llm/analyze-player
  • POST /api/llm/compare-players
  • GET /api/llm/card-analysis
